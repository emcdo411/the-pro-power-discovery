## 🧠 Executive Context

This repository is the engagement artifact for a **structured operational diagnostic** of Pro Power Support — a field-service and generator maintenance business — conducted by **Epoch Frameworks LLC** under the **DARE Operational Resilience framework**.

> Most field-service operations have capable crews, working equipment, and customer relationships.  
> Few have **operational memory, decision architecture, or AI-ready workflow infrastructure.**

This engagement closes that gap.

---

## ⚠️ The Core Operational Problem

Pro Power Support operates in a **high-stakes, low-margin, execution-dependent** environment:

- Field crews respond to equipment failures
- Proposals are built on fragmented data
- Inspection visibility lives in disconnected systems
- Decisions are founder-dependent
- AI adoption is blocked by process debt — not technology

The DARE framework surfaces where the friction lives, scores the operational exposure, and designs the corrective architecture.

---

## 🔍 What the DARE Framework Diagnoses

**DARE** is a four-dimension operational stress-test built for field-service and infrastructure companies:

| Dimension | What It Interrogates |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **D — Data** | Signal integrity, reporting reliability, system fragmentation, proposal data alignment, equipment/inspection visibility |
| **A — Agility** | Deployment speed, mobile unit coordination, field escalation loops, proposal turnaround, decision latency |
| **R — Risk** | Hidden operational exposure, compliance vulnerabilities, contract margin risk, maintenance failure patterns, dependency concentration |
| **E — Evolution** | Scalability readiness, AI integration fit, process maturity, operational memory, founder dependency reduction |
