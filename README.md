## What This Repository Is

This repository contains the pre-engagement operational intelligence brief built for Pro Power Support — an energy infrastructure, inspection, and field services company operating across the United States. The analysis was built from confirmed operational architecture and public company documentation, not a template.

The central finding is consistent across industrial operators of this type: the data exists, the capability exists, the people exist. What does not exist is a structured system for converting operational signals into decisions before they become failures.

DARE — the Operational Stress-Test and Decision Intelligence System coined by McDonald (2026) — is the diagnostic framework applied here. It evaluates four operational dimensions: Data signal integrity, Agility under execution pressure, Risk accumulation across workflows, and Evolution readiness as the organization scales.

---

## The Core Problem

Most infrastructure companies have data. Very few have operational intelligence.

The distinction matters. Data tells leadership what happened. Operational intelligence tells leadership what is about to happen, where execution is degrading, and which decisions are being made from signals that no longer reflect current conditions. In field-service organizations, that gap between data and intelligence is where downtime accumulates, margin erodes, and scaling creates instability rather than capacity.

This brief identifies where that gap exists inside Pro Power Support's current operational architecture and what closing it would produce.

---

## DARE Framework Application

DARE is positioned not as a consulting framework but as an operational stress-test. Each dimension interrogates a specific failure mode that field-service and energy infrastructure organizations consistently produce at scale.

### D — Data

**"What signals is leadership making decisions from?"**

This layer evaluates whether operational data is trustworthy, timely, and connected across the business.

### A — Agility

**"How fast can the organization adapt operationally?"**

This dimension evaluates execution responsiveness — how quickly the organization can move from a decision to an action.

### R — Risk

**"Where is hidden operational exposure accumulating?"**

This is the highest-value layer of the DARE assessment. Risk accumulates invisibly in field-service operations because it does not register as risk until it becomes an event.

### E — Evolution

**"Can the organization scale without operational instability?"**

This is where most field-service organizations fail. Growth does not automatically produce operational clarity.
