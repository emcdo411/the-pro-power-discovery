# Operator Ownership Model

## Purpose

The Operator Ownership Model defines who owns each operational decision, which signals trigger action, and how accountability is verified. For Pro Power Support, this model is especially important because field-service and infrastructure businesses often rely on experienced people who know what to do, but have not converted that knowledge into repeatable operating structure.

## Core Problem

When ownership is informal, execution depends on memory, personality, and urgency. That may work when the company is smaller, but it becomes fragile as the business scales across more customers, contracts, equipment classes, mobile deployments, inspections, and government opportunities.

## Ownership Categories

### Executive Ownership

Responsible for strategic decisions and operating priorities: growth priorities, government contracting direction, large infrastructure opportunity decisions, major capital decisions, strategic partnership approvals.

### Operational Ownership

Responsible for daily execution and coordination: dispatch decisions, crew assignments, mobile unit deployment, project sequencing, field escalation, equipment availability.

### Technical Ownership

Responsible for equipment performance, inspection quality, and technical reliability: generator inspection, turbine system status, diesel generation readiness, maintenance findings, failure analysis.

### Commercial Ownership

Responsible for proposal quality, bid readiness, customer communication, and margin protection: RFP intake, pricing assumptions, proposal language, scope exclusions, government contract compliance packaging.

### AI / System Ownership

Responsible for validating whether AI outputs are useful, accurate, timely, and aligned with real workflows: AI-generated proposal drafts, RFP summaries, inspection trend summaries, operational alerts, anomaly detection outputs.

## Ownership Table

| Workflow | Primary Owner | Trigger | Required Evidence | Escalation Point | Closure Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RFP review | Commercial lead | New bid received | Requirements summary | Missing compliance item | Go / no-go decision documented |
| Mobile unit deployment | Operations lead | Customer emergency or scheduled need | Unit availability and crew status | Equipment conflict or delay | Deployment confirmed |
| Inspection follow-up | Technical lead | Finding logged | Inspection notes and severity | Repeat finding or safety concern | Corrective action assigned |
| Proposal pricing | Commercial + operations | New proposal request | Labor, equipment, material assumptions | Margin uncertainty | Priced scope approved |
| AI output review | Assigned workflow owner | AI recommendation generated | Source evidence and confidence | Conflict with field judgment | Accepted, revised, or rejected |

## Desired Outcome

The model should make it clear: who owns the decision, what starts the decision, what evidence is needed, when escalation happens, and how closure is verified.

## Strategic Value

A clear operator ownership model allows Pro Power Support to scale without turning every operational uncertainty into an owner-level interruption.
