# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Purpose:** Pre-engagement discovery, operational intelligence assessment, AI workflow evaluation, and potential advisory engagement between Epoch Frameworks LLC and Pro Power Support or its authorized representatives.

## 1. Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is intended for use between:

- **Disclosing / Receiving Party:** Epoch Frameworks LLC, Fort Worth, Texas
- **Disclosing / Receiving Party:** Pro Power Support, its affiliates, owners, officers, employees, contractors, or authorized representatives

Each party may disclose confidential business, technical, operational, financial, customer, vendor, pricing, contract, process, system, or strategic information to the other.

## 2. Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

- customer lists, contract opportunities, government contracting pipelines, bid strategies, and RFP materials
- field-service procedures, dispatch logic, inspection processes, equipment utilization data, maintenance records, and operational workflows
- software, systems, dashboards, reports, AI tooling, internal data structures, and system architecture
- financial, pricing, labor, vendor, equipment, margin, and proposal information
- business strategy, growth plans, expansion targets, mobile unit deployment plans, and executive decision criteria
- Epoch Frameworks LLC diagnostic methods, scoring instruments, DARE framework logic, AI Adoption Architect logic, decision architecture models, and proprietary deliverables

Confidential Information may be written, verbal, electronic, visual, operational, or inferred through discovery conversations.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- was publicly available without breach of this Agreement
- was already known to the receiving party before disclosure
- was independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information
- was rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality
- must be disclosed by law, subpoena, court order, or government process

## 4. Use Limitation

Confidential Information may only be used to evaluate, scope, negotiate, or perform a potential advisory, diagnostic, operational intelligence, AI adoption, or implementation engagement between the parties.

## 5. Protection Obligations

Each party agrees to:

- protect Confidential Information using reasonable care
- limit access to personnel, contractors, advisors, or legal representatives with a legitimate need to know
- prevent unauthorized disclosure, publication, copying, or transfer
- promptly notify the disclosing party of any suspected unauthorized access or disclosure

## 6. Proprietary Framework Protection

The receiving party acknowledges that Epoch Frameworks LLC frameworks, including but not limited to DARE, AI Adoption Architect v6.6, Criteria Evaluation System, Human-Centered Decision Gate, Chameleon Governance Model, operator ownership models, diagnostic instruments, scoring systems, and decision architecture logic, are proprietary intellectual property.

Disclosure of presentation-layer materials, GitHub Pages briefs, sample diagnostics, or engagement documents does not grant ownership, license, reuse rights, training rights, derivative rights, resale rights, or implementation rights unless expressly granted in a signed written agreement.

## 7. No Obligation to Proceed

This Agreement does not require either party to enter into a transaction, statement of work, partnership, employment relationship, vendor relationship, or consulting engagement.

## 8. Return or Destruction

Upon request, each party will return or destroy Confidential Information in its possession, except for archival copies maintained for legal, compliance, backup, or professional recordkeeping purposes.

## 9. Term

The confidentiality obligations begin on the effective date of signature and remain in effect for three years.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## 11. Equitable Relief

Unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm. The injured party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedies.
