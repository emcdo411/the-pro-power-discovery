## Purpose

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement governs the exchange of confidential information in connection with pre-engagement discovery, operational intelligence assessment, AI workflow evaluation, and any potential advisory engagement between the parties, including any engagement governed by SOW-PP-2026-001.

---

## 1. Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

**From Pro Power Support:**
- Customer lists, contract opportunities, government contracting pipelines, bid strategies, and RFP materials
- Field-service procedures, dispatch logic, inspection processes, equipment utilization data, maintenance records, and operational workflows
- Software, systems, dashboards, reports, AI tooling, internal data structures, and system architecture
- Financial, pricing, labor, vendor, equipment, margin, and proposal information
- Business strategy, growth plans, expansion targets, mobile unit deployment plans, and executive decision criteria

**From Epoch Frameworks LLC:**
- Diagnostic methods, scoring instruments, and DARE framework logic
- AI Adoption Architect methodology, Criteria Evaluation System, Human-Centered Decision Gate, and Chameleon Governance Model
- Operator ownership models, decision architecture models, and proprietary analytical logic
- Client engagement structures, deliverable formats, and framework IP protected under DACR License v2.6

Confidential Information may be written, verbal, electronic, visual, operational, or inferred through discovery conversations, whether or not marked "confidential" at the time of disclosure.

---

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

1. Was publicly available at the time of disclosure without breach of this Agreement
2. Was already known to the receiving party prior to disclosure, as documented in records predating this Agreement
3. Was independently developed by the receiving party without use of or reference to the disclosing party's Confidential Information
4. Was rightfully received from a third party under no duty of confidentiality to the disclosing party
5. Must be disclosed by applicable law, subpoena, court order, or government process — provided the receiving party gives prompt written notice to the disclosing party before disclosure, to the extent permitted by law

---

## 3. Use Limitation

Confidential Information received under this Agreement may only be used to evaluate, scope, negotiate, or perform a potential advisory, diagnostic, operational intelligence, AI adoption, or implementation engagement between the parties.

Confidential Information may not be used to:

- Develop competing products, services, methodologies, or frameworks
- Train AI models, machine learning systems, or automated decision tools
- Reproduce, sublicense, or resell any portion of either party's proprietary materials
- Support any third-party engagement without prior written consent

---

## 4. Protection Obligations

Each receiving party agrees to:

- Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care
- Limit access to personnel, contractors, advisors, legal representatives, or financial advisors with a legitimate need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement
- Prevent unauthorized disclosure, publication, copying, reproduction, or transfer
- Promptly notify the disclosing party in writing of any known or suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, or breach

---

## 5. Proprietary Framework Protection

The receiving party acknowledges that Epoch Frameworks LLC frameworks — including but not limited to DARE, AI Adoption Architect v6.6, Criteria Evaluation System, Human-Centered Decision Gate, Chameleon Governance Model, operator ownership models, diagnostic instruments, scoring systems, and decision architecture logic — are proprietary intellectual property protected under:

**DACR License v2.6 | McDonald (2026) | Epoch Frameworks LLC**

Disclosure of presentation-layer materials, GitHub Pages briefs, sample diagnostics, engagement documents, or deliverables does not grant:

- Ownership of any underlying framework
- License to reproduce, adapt, or implement any methodology
- Training rights for AI or automated systems
- Derivative rights, resale rights, or sublicensing rights

No rights are granted except those expressly stated in a separately executed, signed written agreement.

---

## 6. No Obligation to Proceed

This Agreement does not require either party to enter into a transaction, statement of work, partnership, employment relationship, vendor relationship, or consulting engagement. Either party may discontinue discussions at any time without liability.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction

Upon written request by the disclosing party, the receiving party will promptly return or certifiably destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control, including copies and derivatives, except for:

- Archival copies maintained for legal, compliance, backup, or professional recordkeeping purposes
- Confidential Information required to be retained by applicable law or regulatory obligation

Destruction certification will be provided in writing upon request.

---

## 8. Term

The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement begin on the Effective Date and remain in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date, or three (3) years from the conclusion of any engagement governed by an associated Statement of Work, whichever is later.

---

## 9. Equitable Relief

Each party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The injured party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or other equitable remedies in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the requirement to post bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or equity.

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
