# Statement of Work

## Engagement Title

**DARE Operational Resilience Assessment and AI Workflow Integration Review**

## Parties

- **Advisor:** Epoch Frameworks LLC
- **Client:** Pro Power Support

## Engagement Objective

The objective of this engagement is to evaluate Pro Power Support's operational architecture, decision flows, field-to-office visibility, AI integration readiness, and execution risk profile using the DARE operational stress-test framework.

## Scope of Work

Epoch Frameworks LLC will perform a structured diagnostic across four DARE dimensions:

1. **Data:** operational signal integrity, reporting reliability, equipment and inspection visibility, proposal data alignment, system fragmentation
2. **Agility:** deployment speed, mobile unit coordination, field escalation loops, proposal turnaround, decision latency
3. **Risk:** hidden operational exposure, compliance vulnerabilities, contract margin risk, maintenance failure patterns, dependency concentration
4. **Evolution:** scalability readiness, AI integration fit, process maturity, operational memory, founder dependency reduction

## Included Activities

- Executive discovery with ownership
- Review of selected operating documents, proposals, inspection workflows, system screenshots, and process notes
- Field-to-office workflow mapping
- Decision latency mapping
- Operational risk matrix development
- AI readiness and workflow integration assessment
- Priority action stack design
- Executive findings brief

## Deliverables

- DARE Operational Resilience Brief
- Operational Risk Matrix
- Decision Latency Map
- Friction Mapping Report
- AI Workflow Integration Assessment
- Priority Action Stack
- Executive recommendations for next-phase implementation

**Structure:** 25 hours per week

**Rate:** $185 per hour

**Duration:** 90 days

### Option C: Custom Advisory Block

Custom advisory blocks may be created for targeted RFP intelligence, proposal workflow design, field-service reporting, or AI integration validation.

## Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to provide reasonable access to relevant context, including leadership interviews, process descriptions, operating constraints, representative workflow examples, and selected proposal or RFP artifacts when available.

## Out of Scope

Unless separately agreed in writing, this engagement does not include custom software development, production AI model deployment, cybersecurity remediation, legal advice, accounting advice, engineering certification, equipment repair, permanent staff augmentation, or procurement of third-party tools.
