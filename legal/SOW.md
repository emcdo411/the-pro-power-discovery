## Engagement Objective

The objective of this engagement is to evaluate Pro Power Support's operational architecture, decision flows, field-to-office visibility, AI integration readiness, and execution risk profile using the DARE operational stress-test framework — and to deliver a prioritized action stack and executive findings brief that enable measurable operational improvement.

---

## Scope of Work

Epoch Frameworks LLC will perform a structured diagnostic across four DARE dimensions. Scope includes, but is not limited to, the following:

### 1. Data
Operational signal integrity, reporting reliability, equipment and inspection visibility, proposal data alignment, system fragmentation.

### 2. Agility
Deployment speed, mobile unit coordination, field escalation loops, proposal turnaround, decision latency.

### 3. Risk
Hidden operational exposure, compliance vulnerabilities, contract margin risk, maintenance failure patterns, dependency concentration.

### 4. Evolution
Scalability readiness, AI integration fit, process maturity, operational memory, founder dependency reduction.

> **Scope Protection:** Scope includes, but is not limited to, the items enumerated above. Supporting analysis, documentation, and executive communication artifacts required to operationalize findings are included within scope at Epoch Frameworks LLC's diagnostic judgment, without requiring a Change Order.

---

## Included Activities

- Executive discovery with ownership
- Review of selected operating documents, proposals, inspection workflows, system screenshots, and process notes
- Field-to-office workflow mapping
- Decision latency mapping
- Operational risk matrix development
- AI readiness and workflow integration assessment
- Priority action stack design
- Executive findings brief
