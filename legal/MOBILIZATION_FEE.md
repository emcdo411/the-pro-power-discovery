# Mobilization Fee Structure

## Purpose

The mobilization fee reserves calendar capacity, initiates discovery preparation, protects proprietary framework deployment, and establishes a professional operating boundary before deeper operational review begins.

This fee is not positioned as a technology implementation charge. It is the activation point for a structured operational intelligence engagement.

## Recommended Structure

**Mobilization Fee:** $6,000

**Due:** Upon acceptance of engagement terms and before kickoff scheduling

**Applied Toward:** Diagnostic preparation, discovery setup, framework calibration, initial materials review, stakeholder question design, and operating cadence setup

**Non-Refundable:** Once kickoff preparation begins

## What the Mobilization Fee Covers

- Review of available operating materials, proposals, system screenshots, workflow documents, service categories, equipment categories, and public-facing company information
- Preparation of DARE discovery map
- Stakeholder interview design
- Initial friction hypothesis
- Diagnostic scope confirmation
- Confidentiality and document-control setup
- Advisory calendar reservation
- First executive discovery session

## Why This Matters

Operational intelligence work requires more than a meeting. It requires preparation, pattern recognition, and framework calibration before the first formal diagnostic conversation. The mobilization fee creates mutual commitment and ensures the work begins with seriousness, not casual advisory drift.

## Optional Expansion

If the engagement converts into a 30-day diagnostic sprint or 90-day fractional advisory engagement, the mobilization fee may be credited in whole or in part at Epoch Frameworks LLC discretion.
